Coupons take off too much and tax is calculated wrongly

Two problems with order totals:

1. A 10% coupon takes off about 19%, because the discount gets applied more than once.
2. Tax is charged on the price before the discount. It should be charged on the discounted subtotal.

For items costing 10 and 20 with a 10% coupon and the 10% tax rate, the subtotal is 27.00 and the total is 29.70. With no coupon the same order totals 33.00. Totals are rounded to 2 decimal places.
